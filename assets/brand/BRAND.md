# NepaliTools brand

## The mark

The mark is a calligraphic ने, the first syllable of नेपाली. A single ribbon sweeps
into the headstroke, turns down the stem, and curls into a spiral foot; the e matra
loops at the top right. A crimson ribbon runs inside the cream strokes, so the two
colours read as one brush.

The counters are indigo, the same value as the field. That is why the mark lives on
indigo: the negative space is part of the drawing.

`nepalitools-mark.svg` is the vector master. The PNGs are exports from it.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Indigo | #12233F | Field, counters, wordmark on light backgrounds |
| Cream | #F7F2E8 | The letter, light backgrounds |
| Crimson | #DC143C | The inner ribbon, rules, the small accent |

Three values carry the brand. The social card adds two muted blue-greys for
tertiary text: #4A5A75 for the tagline and #8A97AB for the URL.

## Rules

- Keep the mark on indigo #12233F. Its counters are filled with that value and will
  show on any other background.
- Keep clear space of at least 10 percent of the mark's height on every side.
- Minimum size is 32px for the full mark. At 16px only the silhouette survives,
  which is enough for a favicon but not for a signature.
- Do not set the mark in a font, redraw it, stretch it, rotate it, outline it or add
  a gradient.
- Do not change the three fills.

## Where the logo is set

- Organization avatar: GitHub, organization Settings, Profile, Avatar. Upload
  `nepalitools-mark-1024.png`. The REST API has no avatar endpoint.
- Repository social preview: repository Settings, Social preview, Upload image.
  Upload `nepalitools-social-1280x640.png`.
- README: `nepalitools-mark-512.png` at 96px.
